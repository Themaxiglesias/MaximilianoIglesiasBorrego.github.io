I'm MAXIMILIANO IGLESIAS BORREGO — a Mechanical Engineering student at Michigan State University, graduating May 2027. I care about designing things that work in the real world, learning from testing, and bringing people together to make ambitious ideas happen.

01 / MY PATH

Engineering across
industries.

My engineering experience spans avionics at GE Aerospace, mechanical projects at Marathon Petroleum, and powertrain manufacturing at Ford Motor Company. Each environment has strengthened a different part of my approach: detailed CAD design, systems thinking, manufacturing practicality, and engineering validation.

I'm especially interested in mechanical design, electromechanical hardware, prototyping, and product development — taking an idea from early concept into something that can be built, evaluated, and improved.

03 / WHERE IT STARTED

Hands-on roots.
Engineering mindset.

Long before my internships, I spent time around my family's flooring business, ProntoFlooring. Working around job sites, tools, materials, and the day-to-day realities of getting a job done shaped how I think about engineering.

That experience taught me to pay attention to details that drawings don't always capture: how something gets installed, how people work with it, and what it takes to turn a plan into a finished result.

It's a perspective I carry into design, prototyping, and working with engineering teams today.

04 / LEADERSHIP

More than

I've served two consecutive terms as President of the Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers (SHPE) chapter at Michigan State University. That experience taught me how to organize teams, build partnerships, and create opportunities for students navigating their own engineering journeys.
